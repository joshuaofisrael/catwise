Not a substitute for veterinary care

Our guides, blog posts and tools are general information. They are not a diagnosis, treatment plan or professional advice, and they cannot replace an examination by a veterinarian who knows your cat. If your cat seems unwell, or may have eaten or touched something toxic, call your vet or a pet poison helpline now.

No veterinarian client relationship

Reading MeowWise, using our tools or emailing us does not create a veterinarian client patient relationship with anyone. The MeowWise team are not your veterinarians, and we cannot give advice about an individual animal.

Accuracy

We base our pages on reputable sources such as veterinary schools, veterinary associations, poison helplines and government agencies, and we list those sources on each page. Even so, research and guidance change, and errors can happen. We do not guarantee that any page is complete or current. The toxic plants and household items checker lists only items we could source; an item that is missing is not necessarily safe. Please report mistakes to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.
