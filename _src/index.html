Four facts every cat owner should know

  • Lilies can kill cats. True lilies and daylilies can cause fatal kidney failure, even from pollen or vase water. Lilies and cats.
  • A cat straining to pee and passing little or nothing is an emergency, especially a male cat. Why urinary blockage cannot wait.
  • Cats are obligate carnivores. They rely on nutrients found only in animal products, such as taurine. Cat nutrition.
  • Vets class cats over 10 as seniors and recommend checkups at least every six months from then. Senior cat care.
