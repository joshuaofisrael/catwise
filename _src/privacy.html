CatWise does not ask you to create an account, does not sell personal information and does not use advertising cookies. This page explains the small amount of data involved when you visit or contact us. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who we are

CatWise is operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is privacy focused: it does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not collect personal data such as names or email addresses. We use it only to understand which pages are useful.

Contact form and email

If you use our contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service, and arrive in our email inbox. If you email us directly, we receive your message and email address. We use these details only to reply to you, and do not add you to any mailing list.

Advertising and links

CatWise has no advertising or affiliate links at present. If we add them, we will update this policy first. Our pages link to external sources such as veterinary schools and journals; their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any messages you have sent by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.
