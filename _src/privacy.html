This policy explains what information is involved when you visit MeowWise or contact us, and how we use it. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not sell personal information and we do not use advertising, affiliate or analytics cookies. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who is responsible

MeowWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller responsible for your information. Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

Contact form and email

If you use our contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service, and arrive in our email inbox. If you email us directly, we receive your message and email address. We use these details only to read and reply to your message. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your details for marketing.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in server logs for security and operation. We do not receive or use these logs to identify you. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Fonts

Headings use the free Fredoka font loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. MeowWise does not set Google cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

MeowWise does not currently use analytics, advertising or affiliate tracking, and sets no cookies. If we add analytics in the future, such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, or any advertising or affiliate links, we will update this policy before or when that happens.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to outside sources such as veterinary schools, poison helplines and journals. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Keeping and deleting messages

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete messages you have sent, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and the date at the top will show the latest version. See also our terms of use and disclaimer.
