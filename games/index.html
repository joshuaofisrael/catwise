MeowWise cat games

Two free, original cat games that run right in your browser. Each one teaches a real, sourced cat fact. No sign up, no chat, no ads and no personal data collected; high scores stay only in your own browser.

Read the Cat

A cat body language quiz. Read the tail, ears and body of a cartoon cat and pick what it is telling you. Based on International Cat Care and Cats Protection guidance.

Whisker Pairs

A memory match game with original cat drawings. Every pair you find unlocks a true cat fact with its source.

For grown ups

Games work with mouse, touch and keyboard, play no sound and respect reduced motion settings. Teachers can use them as a warm up or reward; see MeowWise for teachers.
