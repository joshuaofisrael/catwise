Tail Talk: what is this cat saying?

Look at the cartoon cat, then pick what it is most likely telling you. After each answer you get the real fact behind it. Five rounds; your best score is saved only in this browser.

Cartoon cats are simplified. Real cats combine many signals, so always look at the whole cat and the situation. This game is education only.
