Cat science vocabulary list

By the MeowWise team · Published 8 October 2026 · Last reviewed 8 October 2026

Free to print for classroom use. No sign up, no ads.

Plain language definitions of the science words students meet when they learn about cats. Print it as a study sheet or cut it up for a word wall. For vet and breed terms such as HCM, PKD and AAFCO, see our full cat glossary.

Grades 3 to 5

Adaptation
A body part or behavior that helps a living thing survive where it lives. A cat's whiskers are an adaptation for feeling its way in the dark.
Carnivore
An animal that eats other animals. Cats are carnivores.
Function
The job a body part does.
Inherited trait
A trait passed from parents to their young, such as fur color.
Kitten
A young cat.
Litter
A group of kittens born to the same mother at the same time.
Offspring
The young of a living thing. Kittens are a cat's offspring.
Predator
An animal that hunts other animals for food.
Prey
An animal that is hunted for food.
Sense
A way an animal takes in information: sight, hearing, smell, taste or touch.
Structure
A part of a living thing, inside or outside its body, such as ears, whiskers or a heart.
Trait
A feature of a living thing, such as fur length or ear shape.
Variation
Differences between living things of the same kind, like kittens in one litter with different colored fur.
Vibrissae
The scientific name for whiskers: thick, sensitive hairs that feel touch and air movement.

Grades 6 to 12

Artificial selection
When people choose which animals breed so that a wanted trait becomes more common. Cat breeds are a result of artificial selection.
Breed
A group of cats bred by people to share a set of traits, such as the Maine Coon or Siamese. See our breed guide.
Domestication
The long process by which a wild species comes to live with and depend on people. House cats were domesticated from the Near Eastern wildcat.
Dominant variant
A gene version that shows its effect even when an animal has only one copy.
Gene
A section of DNA that carries instructions for a trait.
Genome
All of the DNA of a living thing.
Obligate carnivore
An animal that must eat animal tissue to get all the nutrients it needs. Cats are obligate carnivores. See glossary.
Pheromone
A chemical signal an animal gives off that other animals of the same species can detect.
Pseudogene
A gene that no longer works because of changes in its DNA. The cat's sweet taste gene Tas1r2 is a pseudogene.
Queen
An unneutered female cat. An unneutered male is called a tom. See glossary.
Socialization period
The early weeks of a kitten's life, roughly 2 to 7 weeks, when gentle experiences teach it what is normal and safe. See kittens.
Taurine
A nutrient found in meat that cats must get from their food. Too little can cause heart and eye problems. See glossary.
Wildcat
Felis silvestris, the wild species group that house cats came from.
