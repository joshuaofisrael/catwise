Is it toxic to cats? Plant, food and household poison checker

By the CatWise team · Last updated 8 October 2026

Type a plant, food or household product to check whether it is toxic to cats. The most dangerous items for cats are true lilies and daylilies, sago palm, foxglove, lily of the valley, human painkillers, dog flea treatments containing permethrin, antifreeze and rat poison: if your cat may have touched or eaten any of these, contact a vet immediately. Every entry links to its source.

If your cat has eaten something toxic

  1. Remove the cat from the source and keep the packaging or plant (or a photo).
  2. Call your vet, an emergency vet or a pet poison service now; in the US, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is on (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply).
  3. Do not make your cat vomit or give home remedies unless a vet tells you to.

Cat poison FAQ

What is the most poisonous plant for cats?

True lilies (such as Easter, tiger, Asiatic and Stargazer lilies) and daylilies are among the most dangerous, because a tiny amount, even pollen or vase water, can cause fatal kidney failure. Sago palm, foxglove and lily of the valley can also be fatal.

Are peace lilies poisonous to cats?

Peace lilies are not true lilies and do not cause kidney failure, but the ASPCA lists them as toxic to cats because they cause mouth irritation, drooling, vomiting and difficulty swallowing.

Is garlic toxic to cats?

Yes. Garlic, onions and chives, including powders, can damage red blood cells and cause anemia, and the ASPCA notes cats are more susceptible than dogs.

Which houseplants are safe for cats?

The ASPCA lists spider plant, Boston fern, African violet and areca palm, among many others, as non toxic to cats. Check any plant in the ASPCA database before bringing it home.

What should I do if my cat ate something poisonous?

Contact your vet, an emergency vet or a pet poison service immediately, and keep the packaging or plant. Do not induce vomiting or give home remedies unless a vet advises it.
